The Chronicle of Shandalar

The Great Barrier

Shandalar is a small plane rich in living mana, magic so strong it seems almost aware. Long ago the necromancer Lim-Dul tried to make it his own, and he was stopped by Kenan Sahrmal, Shandalar's greatest hero, and his pupil Azaar.

Azaar's last spell was meant to raise a temporary ward around the plane. Instead it caught Lim-Dul's spirit and his own in Azaar's body, and drew on both to raise the Great Barrier, a wall that no planeswalker could break. As long as Lim-Dul stays imprisoned, the barrier holds.

Sahrmal gave the last of his power to his second apprentice, who became the immortal Guardian. With the plane's greatest wizards, the Guardian founded five guilds, one for each colour of magic, to teach it and keep it from being abused.

Arzakon

Far across the multiverse, a planeswalker named Arzakon felt the echoes of Shandalar's wars and came looking for their source. The Guardian threw it back at the barrier. Arzakon did not give up. Over many years it slipped part of its spirit through and began to whisper to the Guild Lords.

At a conclave called over some small dispute, the five Lords rose as one and struck the Guardian down. Arzakon then set them a race: whoever gathers enough magic will learn the Spell of Dominion and rule Shandalar forever.

The Spell of Dominion will do nothing more than seek out the source of the Great Barrier … and destroy it.

An old oneiromancer, touched by what is left of the Guardian's spirit, has seen the truth in her dreams. The first person she could convince was a wandering amateur spellcaster: you.

Your journey

You start with a weak deck, a little gold and a lot of land to cross. On the way you will find:

  • Towns and villages that sell cards and food, offer quests and ask for help
  • Wandering creatures sent by the Guild Lords, each with its own deck
  • Dungeons full of monsters and rare cards
  • Castles where the Guild Lords wait

Every fight is a game of Magic played for ante. Beat all five Lords before their minions take over Shandalar's towns, and then face Arzakon itself.
