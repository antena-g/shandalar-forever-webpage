The Chronicle of Shandalar

History Arzakon Guild Lords Lands Places World Magics

A plane drowning in mana

Shandalar is a small plane with more magic than it knows what to do with. Its mana is so rich that wizards of old made creatures out of pure mana, and their descendants, the great dragons among them, still roam the land. Lesser spells are part of everyday life, even for ordinary folk.

For ages the plane prospered alone, watched over by Kenan Sahrmal, a planeswalker who chose to protect his home rather than prey on others.

Lim-Dul's War

Then the necromancer Lim-Dul arrived. Sahrmal drove off the planeswalkers who came with him, but Lim-Dul struck the weakened hero down with a pitchfork. Sahrmal vanished; Lim-Dul was beheaded. Neither was truly gone.

Twelve years later Lim-Dul returned at the head of an undead army. The war lasted years and laid waste to every great city but one: Ardestan. In secret, Sahrmal had been training two pupils to take his place. One was Azaar; the other's name has never been revealed.

As Ardestan fell, Azaar ran to its defence with a great spell stolen from his teacher. In the last moment Lim-Dul leapt into Azaar's body, and the spell, meant to imprison the necromancer, trapped both spirits in one body. Their imprisoned power raised the Great Barrier, a wall no planeswalker could break, for as long as Lim-Dul stays bound.

The Guardian and the guilds

Sahrmal gave the last of his power to his second pupil, who became the immortal Guardian of Shandalar. With the plane's greatest wizards, the Guardian founded five guilds, one for each colour of magic, to teach it and keep it from being abused. Each guild is led by a Guild Lord, the champion of a great contest.

In time Lim-Dul won the struggle inside Azaar's body and rose again, and was beaten again in the Wizards' War, at the cost of the Black Guild's Lord. The Guardian sealed his spirit in an artifact. Azaar's empty body, steeped in magic, would not lie still: it rose as the undead Lichlord and took command of the Black Guild.

Arzakon

Far across the multiverse, a planeswalker named Arzakon felt the echoes of Shandalar's wars and came looking for their source. The Guardian threw it back at the barrier. Arzakon did not give up. Over many years it slipped part of its spirit through and began to whisper to the Guild Lords.

At a conclave called over some small dispute, the five Lords rose as one and struck the Guardian down. Arzakon then set them a race: whoever gathers enough magic will learn the Spell of Dominion and rule Shandalar forever.

The Spell of Dominion will do nothing more than seek out the source of the Great Barrier … and destroy it.

An old oneiromancer, touched by what is left of the Guardian's spirit, has seen the truth in her dreams. The first person she could convince was a wandering amateur spellcaster: you.

The lands

Shandalar's continent has five kinds of land, one for each colour of magic, all ringed by the great ocean Tonamur, which no one can cross. Each land is home to creatures of its colour, and villages trade mostly in the local magic. Roads speed you through any of them.

Mana links and Mana Domes

The minor wizards' cities are Shandalar's richest untapped stores of magic. Every mana link you hold with a city adds one life to your total in every duel. You start with a few and earn more by finishing quests.

The Guild Lords send their creatures to conquer those same cities. A conquered city disappears under a Mana Dome in its conqueror's colour, your link to it is lost, and the Lord gains a mana tap. A Lord with enough taps can cast the Spell of Dominion. Defeat the dome's guardian to set the city free.
