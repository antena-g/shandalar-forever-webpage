From the game's own creature table and decks

Bestiary

Each of the five Guild Lords has unleashed a horde of magical minions. They stop anyone they meet and demand a duel, or payment for the right to cross the land their master claims.

Facing a creature

When a creature catches you, both of you put a card up as ante. You can duel it, pay it off if it will take a bribe, or try to answer its riddle, usually a question about Magic cards. A wrong answer costs you your ante.

Beat a creature and you choose your reward: its ante, or what it knows. Defeated creatures are the only way to learn where hidden dungeons lie and what waits inside the five castles. Every minion you defeat also costs its master life.

Creatures rarely stray far from their home terrain. Many also adjust their decks to you, slipping in a few extra cards against the colour you play.
